Welcome to Star Group. Imagine this is your first week here as a junior developer. You are still getting familiar with the company, the team and the systems people use every day. Star Group operates different kinds of vehicles, private cars, micro buses, ambulances and delivery vans. But much of the information about those vehicles is still maintained manually in spreadsheets. Adding a new vehicle, checking an existing record or correcting information means going back to those files again and again. It works, but it is hardly convenient. One morning, the head of administration comes to the IT team with a straightforward request. We need a simple way to manage our vehicle records. They are not asking for a huge enterprise platform. They want a small application that solves the immediate need and works properly. The senior developers are already focused on larger projects, so your team lead turns to you and says, keep it simple, follow the requirement, use AI and build a working version.
